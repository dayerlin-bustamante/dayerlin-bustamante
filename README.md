## 👩‍💻 About me

I have 5+ years of experience designing scalable frontend architectures with **Angular (Signals, RxJS)**, enterprise **Design Systems** and modern interaction pipelines. Today I focus on the intersection of **Applied AI and reactive interfaces**: AI agents for engineering workflows, real-time inference streaming (SSE) and **Generative UI** components with strict contract validation.

I've led high-availability migrations (Angular 6 → 17 on a platform with millions of monthly users) and automated testing and CI/CD flows. I care about accessible, high-performance and maintainable UIs that help teams ship consistent product experiences at scale.
